You have hundreds of threads. Nobody has shown you how to weave them together.

Teachers. Doctors. Therapists. Evaluations. School meetings. Google searches. Social media. Family. And, of course, years of knowing your own child.

So why don't you know what to do?

Your child's biggest struggle right now isn't the whole picture of your child.

The first step toward knowing what to do is seeing how it all weaves together.

That's what the Child Compass is for.

Who this is for

Made for parents who know something isn't working, and are overwhelmed by what they're supposed to do about it.

This masterclass is for you if:

  • You're raising a child with unique needs: big emotions, sensory needs, learning differences, executive functioning or school struggles.
  • You have a diagnosis, or you don't have one yet.
  • You're drowning in information and want to see how it all connects.
  • You'd like to know where to focus first, instead of trying everything at once.

It's probably not for you if:

  • You're hoping for a quick fix for one behavior in an hour.
  • You want a 47-item to-do list of things to try.
  • You're looking for medical or therapeutic advice. This webinar will focus on educational assistance and home support and is not intended as a replacement for medical, legal, or therapeutic advice.
What you'll learn

Five questions that help you see your child more clearly.

We're not trying to solve everything in one session…
we're looking for clues, patterns and the places where something suddenly makes more sense.

North · Who is my child?

Strengths

Start with who's in front of you, not with what's wrong. Strengths are quiet, and they tell you what you can build on.

East · How does my child experience the world?

Regulation

Learn why your child can seem like a different kid on different days, and why triggers are information.

South · Where does my child need support?

Areas of Opportunity

Move from "what's wrong?" to the specific places where more understanding, practice or support may help.

West · What support will help my child thrive?

Supports

Think beyond "I need to find another expert" to the supports that match what your child actually needs.

Center · Where do we go from here?

Next Steps

Is it really free?

Yes. The masterclass is totally free and all you need is something to write your notes in. You'll also have the opportunity when you sign up to purchase the optional Child Compass Organizer for $17. It's a “nice to have” to go deeper and refer to later, but not required.

Will you be selling something?

At the end, I'll tell you about my membership, the Empowered Parent Collective, for parents who want practical help with what the Compass reveals. There's no pressure whatsoever. In fact, the masterclass is useful on its own as stand-alone support.

Does my child need a diagnosis?

No. The Child Compass works for kids who have a diagnosis, are “quirky” or just have unique needs you don't feel equipped to figure out as a parent.

The recording will be sent to everyone who registers, but attending live means you'll get the chance to ask live questions about the issues you're having with your child in real time. The replay will be available for a limited time of 7 days.

Common questions

Is EPC only for parents of kids with IEPs or diagnoses?

No. EPC supports parents raising kids who don't fit the mold, diagnosed or not.

I'm already overwhelmed. Will this feel like one more thing?

No. Everything is self-paced, and the resources are short and focused. Support is there when you need it.

Is this on Facebook or social media?

No. EPC is hosted on Mighty Networks, a private, secure, distraction-free platform.

Do I get access right away?

You'll get access to the resources straight away, and to the community once you've answered a few quick questions.

Can I cancel?

Yes, you can cancel anytime. If it's within the 30-day refund period, you'll receive a full refund within 24 hours of cancellation. If it's outside of that, your cancellation will happen at the next renewal date, whether that's monthly or annually (that depends on what you chose when you joined).

Does EPC replace professional support?

No. EPC focuses on educational assistance and home support, and it isn't intended as a replacement for medical, legal or therapeutic advice. It helps you work out what support your child needs and how to get it.

What if my family needs more individual help?

Private, custom support with Lisa is available separately for families who need more individualized help. To ask about it, email lisacorricello@gmail.com.

What if it's not the right fit?

You're covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee and can cancel anytime.
